# HL Intel Outreach Kit

## Goal

Sell the first 5 Pro seats before adding more product surface.

The pitch is not "copy whales." The pitch is:

> filtered Hyperliquid wallet intelligence for traders who already manage their own execution.

## Best Early Buyers

- Hyperliquid perp traders
- Crypto Telegram traders who already pay for tools or private groups
- Traders posting market structure, perp OI, funding, or wallet-flow analysis
- Small trading groups that want cleaner signal in one private channel

Avoid:

- beginners asking for entries
- people demanding guaranteed PnL
- anyone who wants managed funds, custody, or auto-trading

## Objection Replies

### "Is this signals?"

Not in the entry/exit sense. It is market intelligence: tracked wallet movement, size, side, profile, and context. You still make your own trade decisions.

### "Why would this have edge?"

The edge is filtering and speed. Raw wallet activity is noisy. HL Intel removes obvious bot/MM behavior and compresses the remaining movement into something a trader can review quickly.

### "Can I copy the wallets?"

I would not market it that way. Some wallets are not copyable in practice because of timing, sizing, or behavior. The product is context, not blind replication.

### "What if there are no alerts?"

Then the feed should be quiet. Flooding low-quality movement would make the product worse.

### "Do you guarantee PnL?"

No. No trade calls, no custody, no execution, no guaranteed outcomes.

## Daily Outreach Target

Send 25 targeted DMs per day for 4 days.

Success threshold:

- 100 DMs sent
- 20 replies

- 2 paid Pro seats

Kill or change the offer if:

- fewer than 5 replies from 100 targeted DMs
- people understand the offer but no one will pay
- every reply asks for guaranteed signals or copy-trading

## Manual Sales Flow

1. Send DM.
2. If they reply, ask what they currently use for Hyperliquid wallet/trader flow.
3. Ask whether they prefer curated wallets, custom watchlists, or a blend of both.
4. Send the page only after they show interest.
5. Invite them to Free if they hesitate.
6. Push Pro only for traders who understand the data-product angle.

## Current Outreach Learning

The broad founder-pilot pitch gets polite declines from some credible accounts.

The stronger angle is workflow-specific:
- rank wallets by quality
- filter by token
- alert on entry/exit
- summarize favorite-wallet activity

Use the workflow-first script for colder accounts and save the founder-pilot language for prospects who already understand the problem.
