# HL Intel Proof Packet - Short Version

HL Intel is a Telegram-first Hyperliquid wallet intelligence feed.

Not signals. Not copy-trading. No custody or execution.

The product tracks what ranked wallets do after the headline move:
- add
- trim
- exit
- flip
- hedge
- approach liquidation pressure
- form confluence across multiple top wallets

## Best Public Example

Chart proof:
https://x.com/HLIntelFeed/status/2057432418684895486

Read:
- 4 top-50 wallets short `$HYPE-PERP`.
- About `$14M` combined exposure.
- HYPE was moving nearly vertical.
- The value was not "short it."
- The value was knowing what to watch next: add, trim, exit, flip, liquidation pressure.

## Why It Is Different

Raw whale trackers show the headline:
- wallet opened long
- wallet opened short
- wallet PnL
- position size

HL Intel tries to answer:
- Is this behavior meaningful or noise?
- Are multiple ranked wallets aligned?
- Are ranked wallets fighting the public narrative?
- Is a wallet adding into stress or reducing exposure?
- Is liquidation risk becoming relevant?
