FAQ

What is Hyperliquid?

A decentralized perpetuals exchange. HL Intel monitors the top leaderboard wallets for market intelligence.

How do you identify algos?

By trading pattern. Wallets trading many assets simultaneously or showing obvious market-maker behavior are flagged and excluded from the Pro feed.

How do I access Pro?

After checkout, you receive access to @HLIntelPro. Cancelled members are removed automatically.

What does Pro track?

Pro tracks exposure changes, whale confluence, wallet reactivation, margin stress, liquidation pressure, open-interest structure, and selected below-threshold positioning.

Do you place trades for me?

No. HL Intel does not provide custody, automated execution, or trade instructions. It is a filtered market intelligence feed.

What if there are no strong alerts?

Quiet is part of the product. The goal is fewer, higher-context alerts instead of flooding Pro with every wallet movement.

Is this financial advice?

No. HL Intel provides market data and wallet intelligence only. Always do your own research and manage your own risk.
