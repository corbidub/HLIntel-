# HL Intel Free - How To Use This Channel

Welcome to HL Intel Free.

This channel shows selected Hyperliquid wallet-intelligence examples, market-structure notes, and digest excerpts. It is designed to help you judge the format before joining Pro.

HL Intel is not a trade-calling service. It is a data feed for traders who already manage their own execution and risk.

## What You Receive

**Funding Rate Spikes**
When funding gets stretched enough to matter. Use it as crowding context, not a standalone trade trigger.

**Open Interest Surges**
When new leverage enters a market. Combine it with price action and wallet flow to read whether exposure is building or unwinding.

**Confluence Teasers**
When multiple tracked wallets align on the same coin and direction. Free may show the broad read; Pro gets wallet ranks, size, risk context, and chart detail.

**Selected Wallet Notes**
Occasional public examples showing how HL Intel reads add, trim, exit, flip, reactivation, hot-streak, or stress behavior.

**Digest Excerpts**
Short summaries of what the paid feed is tracking.

## What Pro Adds

- Filtered tracked-wallet exposure-change alerts.
- Curated/VIP wallet context.
- Custom watch-wallet and token lanes.
- Wallet health alerts: hot streak, cooling off, implosion watch, self-imploding.
- Stress and liquidation-pressure warnings.
- Full confluence reads.
- Weekly digest with the cleanest wallet reads.
