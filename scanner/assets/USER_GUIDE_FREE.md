## How to Use It

**Funding spikes** are most useful as a contrarian signal. Extreme positive funding on a coin = the crowd is long = potential short squeeze setup if price starts fading. Don't trade the signal blindly — use it as context.

**OI surges** tell you when a market is heating up. Rising OI + rising price = trend continuation. Rising OI + falling price = aggressive short selling. Falling OI = positions unwinding.

**Confluence teasers** are the highest-value free alert. When you see one, it means multiple proven human traders independently took the same side. The direction and coin are your signal — even without the full details.
